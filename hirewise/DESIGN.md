# App Design System — HireWise

## Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Clean, modern, and trust-focused, designed to feel professional yet approachable.  
Emphasises clarity, structured content, and confidence through subtle gradients and minimal decoration.

## Typography
- **Headings**: Bold to Extra Bold, 20–32sp  
- **Body text**: Regular, 13–14sp  
- **Labels / captions**: Medium, 11–12sp  
- **Font family**: System default sans-serif  
 



## Component Style
- **Corner radius**: 16–18px for cards, 12–14px for buttons, 8–20px for chips (pill style)  
- **Elevation**: Cards use soft shadow (3–5% opacity, blur 8–12); hero sections have no shadow  
- **Icon style**: Outlined Material icons with contextual colours  
- **Button style**: Full-width for primary CTAs (filled blue); outlined for secondary actions; text buttons for links  



## Layout Principles
- **Horizontal padding**: 16px on all content  
- **Card gap**: 12px between items  
- **Section spacing**: 22–24px between major sections  
- **Bottom navigation**: Always visible; maximum of 4 tabs
