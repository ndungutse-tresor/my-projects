Field Research – HireWise
Date
March 27, 2026
Location
University of Rwanda

Research Method
Each team member conducted:
10–15 minutes of observation
1 short user interview

Participant 1 – Computer Science Student
Observation (10:15 AM)
The student was browsing WhatsApp groups searching for job opportunities. He frequently switched between apps and appeared frustrated.
Friction Points
“I don’t know where to find small jobs quickly.”
“Jobs are shared randomly in WhatsApp groups.”
“Sometimes I message but never get a reply.”
“There is no place to show my skills.”
“I waste a lot of time searching.”



Team Synthesis
Core Problem Statement
Students and local users struggle to efficiently connect with trusted skilled professionals, resulting in wasted time, limited income opportunities, and unreliable service access.

Real-World Constraints
Low Internet Access
Students depend on campus Wi-Fi
Apps must be lightweight
Trust Issues
No verification or rating systems
Time Constraints
Students have limited time to search for work

Problem Selection Rationale
The problem was selected because:
It appeared consistently across all participants
It affects both clients and skilled individuals
It has a clear opportunity for a digital solution
